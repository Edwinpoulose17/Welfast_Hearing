What's Included in Your Hearing Assessment

A diagnostic hearing test is the most accurate way to understand your hearing — and if first-ever hearing aid go far beyond a basic screening will test for:

A full discussion of your hearing

challenges in both quiet and noisy environments.

Video otoscopy – medical examination

of ear canal and eardrum to identify.

Middle-ear testing (tympanometry)

to detect pressure or fluid.

Pure tone audiometry

measuring hearing thresholds across a wide frequency.

Bone conduction testing

to determine the type of hearing loss, if any.

Speech audiometry

assessing how clearly you understand speech in quiet & noise.

A detailed summary

including your audiogram and an action plan for your auditory health.

This takes about 45–60 minutes and is quick assess — most Western take up to 90+ min.

If Hearing Loss Is Detected

We'll guide you through the next steps for your specific hearing profile:

We'll refer you to your GP or ENT specialist if medical care is required.

We'll schedule a follow-up & deliver more detailed a two-part obligation discussion on modern hearing aid technologies and how practical options meet your needs, with no pressure ordering.

Devices Assessed — if hearing aids are required in your care plan, we'll discuss options including behind-the-ear remote models.

Payment options and rebates explained — We'll guide you through Medicare, private health, and other funding options.

We offer complete hearing solutions — from very minimal and discreet to hearing aids, cochlear implants, and assistive listening devices.
